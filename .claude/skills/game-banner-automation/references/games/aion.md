# Aion profile guide

Use actual live Figma dimensions and names as the frame authority. The profile's frame roles are routing hints, not mandatory artboard sizes.

## Brand and hierarchy

- Use the official logo supplied by the campaign, approved live frame, or approved repeat master. The profile does not invent a fixed logo generation or badge.
- Preserve a local title dim and atmospheric detail outside the focal region.
- Default hierarchy is character or item, title, then copy and date unless a user-approved frame establishes another order.
- Named Figma samples control structure and material direction, not their original wording.

## Frame-specific behavior

- Master or Notice carries the complete information hierarchy.
- Facebook or another wide derivative copies finalized role assets but recomposes safe zones rather than scaling the full stack.
- Toast may use a 375×230 contract with `y=0..69` transparent and background only below it when the live document confirms that format. The separately cut character may protrude into the transparent top while remaining inside export bounds.
- EventON receives an independent character crop.
- Ingame protects the upper silhouette and normally omits background-heavy atmosphere.
- List uses a deliberate identity-focused crop at native size.

## Overrides

Live user edits are authoritative. Re-read bounds, font sizes, gaps, crops, role visibility, and layer order before propagation. Never rebuild an approved live title from an older export.
